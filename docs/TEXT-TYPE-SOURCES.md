## Infographic: Physical activity for early years (2019)

- Publisher: UK Department of Health and Social Care; UK Chief Medical Officers’ Physical Activity Guidelines, 2019.
- Exact source file: https://www.cumbria.gov.uk/elibrary/Content/Internet/537/6379/4376214596.pdf
- Publication context: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/physical-activity-guidelines-early-years-under-5s
- Reuse: Crown copyright; Open Government Licence v3.0, as identified by GOV.UK. https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/
- Retrieved 24 September 2026. The main GOV.UK PDF URL now serves a redesigned 2026 version, so do not silently replace this historical example with that download.
- `public/examples/physical-activity-early-years-2019.pdf` is the unchanged one-page source PDF preserved by Cumbria Council. Its metadata identifies DHSC as author and creation in September 2019.
- `public/examples/physical-activity-early-years-2019.png` is the original 1819 × 2573 image extracted from that PDF. No colours, lettering, layout, labels or source line were changed. The on-page close-ups are CSS views of this same image, with the complete image and PDF always available.
- Local discovery: page 65 of `P1 PR 10 — Anthology — FXO2022 Practice Texts.pdf` in OneDrive, `03 IB Lang Lit/01 Paper 1/Practice Papers`. The anthology contains a lower-resolution exam adaptation, including an explanatory tummy-time footnote absent from the original. Our analysis refers only to the original displayed image.
- The text also appears in May 2021 English A: Language and Literature SL Paper 1, TZ2, text 2. This site reproduces the government original under its licence, not the IB paper or marking notes. The guiding question, commentary, models and exercises on the site are original teaching material, not official marked exemplars.
- Further reading linked for students: Purdue OWL, “Visual Rhetoric: Analyzing Visual Documents.” https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/visual_rhetoric/analyzing_visual_documents/index.html
