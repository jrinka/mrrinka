# Paper 2 question-to-thesis practice

## Source

User-supplied `Paper 2 Questions - Master List.xlsx`, sheet `Paper 2 Questions`, rows 6–515. The catalogue covers May 2010–May 2025; no claim is made that this is every historical IB question. Question text and session/year/zone/course/question number come from the workbook. Companion `*_P2_Questions*.txt` files provide original earlier Literature genre headings, which are absent from the spreadsheet despite its introductory note.

`lib/paper-two-questions.json` stores 506 distinct questions, with 510 source references. The donated November 2023 TZ0 Literature copy repeats the four TZ2 combined-course questions. The combined-course spelling and punctuation are used for these duplicates; both references are retained. Whitespace is normalized. Other source wording, including UK spelling, is unchanged. Do not silently modernize or paraphrase these questions.

Default pool: 44 questions from 2023–2025. Earlier pools: 126 general questions and 84 each for drama, poetry, prose fiction and prose non-fiction. General includes older Language & Literature questions and the pre-2013 Literature general section. Genre-specific questions retain their restrictions and are presented as earlier-syllabus practice. The spreadsheet and original exam files are not uploaded.

## Behavior

The activity lives under Practice in both existing Paper 2 course pages; Literature uses the shared Language & Literature guidance. Questions are selected only after an action, avoiding server/client randomness. Selection exhausts each pool before repeating and excludes an immediate repeat. Changing pools retains the others' draw history.

State is kept per question for the current page visit. New questions inherit the current pair of work titles but start fresh evidence/thesis fields. Returning to a previously drawn question restores its own pair and draft. Collapsing the assessment section does not unmount the activity. Refresh or navigation away ends the visit; the UI instructs students to export first. No login, local-storage persistence, AI service, or server submission was added.

TXT/MD exports include all nonempty question records, source labels, both works, evidence, initial thesis and revision. Original question and metadata remain attached to each record. The independent Unpack the question exercise and the comparison refinery keep their original classroom prompts; they are not relabeled as past exam questions.

## Teaching

Use two works by different authors. Examine precise evidence and choices in each, then explain a meaningful relationship that answers the particular question. Similarity, difference, or a more complex relationship may support the claim. No required tripartite thesis, predetermined solution, or automatic mark. The self-check encourages qualification where evidence complicates a reading.
