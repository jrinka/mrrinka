Transitions

Add a related pointfurthermore · in addition · also

Use when the next point develops the same direction.

Introduce a contrasthowever · by contrast · whereas

Name the actual difference; the connector alone does not explain it.

Acknowledge a complicationalthough · nevertheless · even so

Concede something that your argument must account for.

Draw an inferencetherefore · consequently · this suggests

Use only when the second claim follows from the evidence or reasoning.

Offer an examplefor instance · specifically · in particular

Move from a general claim to a precise detail.

Trace developmentinitially · subsequently · by the end

Explain change across a text or argument.

Analysis verbs

contrasts · juxtaposes · frames · withholds · foregrounds · repeats · implies · evokes · questions · undermines · qualifies · exposes

Choose the verb that names the writer’s action accurately. Stronger-sounding words are not automatically better.

Build a connection

Writer → verb → choice and evidence → meaning → explanation.

“The detail suggests… because…” / “Although… initially suggests…, the later… complicates this by…”
