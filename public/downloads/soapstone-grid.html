Reading moveEvidence and thinking
Speaker

Who speaks, in what capacity, and how is authority established? Distinguish a speaker from a publisher.

Occasion

What immediate event and wider circumstances shape the text? Use known context; do not invent it.

Audience

Who is directly addressed? What larger audience might the text reach? Identify clues.

Purpose

What does the speaker invite that audience to think, feel or do? Distinguish this from the subject.

Subject

What does the text literally discuss? Keep this brief so you can move on to its choices.

Tone

Name an attitude precisely, then find its textual basis. Trace changes rather than attaching one adjective to everything.
