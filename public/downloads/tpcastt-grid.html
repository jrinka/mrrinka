Reading moveEvidence and thinking
Title: a first hypothesis

Before reading, what possibilities does the title suggest? Keep the prediction provisional.

Paraphrase

Establish what literally happens. Who speaks? What changes? Summarize briefly in your own words.

Connotation

Select precise language, imagery, sound or form. Explain what each choice adds beyond literal meaning.

Attitude

Describe the speaker’s attitude with evidence. Do not automatically equate the speaker with the poet.

Shifts

Locate a change in voice, tone, imagery, time or argument. What becomes possible after it?

Title: reconsidered

Return to your first hypothesis. How has the whole poem changed your understanding?

Theme

Write a defensible claim about meaning, not a one-word topic. Test it against the whole poem.
